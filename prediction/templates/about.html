🔍 What is PMOS?

Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS) is a common hormonal condition that can affect people with ovaries, particularly during their reproductive years.

PMOS can affect menstrual cycles, hormone levels, metabolism and fertility. The symptoms can vary from person to person.

⚠️ Common Symptoms

  • Irregular or unpredictable menstrual periods.
  • Longer or shorter menstrual cycles.
  • Excess hair growth, especially on the face or body.
  • Acne or oily skin.
  • Hair thinning or hair loss.
  • Weight gain or difficulty managing weight.
  • Changes in hormone levels.
  • Difficulty becoming pregnant in some cases.

🧬 What Causes PMOS?

The exact cause of PMOS is not completely understood. Several factors may contribute to its development.

  • Hormonal imbalance.
  • Insulin resistance.
  • Genetic factors.
  • Family history of PCOS.
  • Lifestyle and metabolic factors.

❤️ How Can PMOS Affect the Body?

  • 🩸 Menstrual Health: Hormonal changes can lead to irregular or absent periods.
  • ⚖️ Metabolic Health: Some people with PMOS may experience insulin resistance or difficulty managing weight.
  • 🧴 Skin & Hair: PMOS may be associated with acne, excess hair growth or hair thinning.
  • 👶 Reproductive Health: PMOS can sometimes affect ovulation and fertility.

🥗 Managing PMOS

PMOS does not have one single treatment that works for everyone. Healthy lifestyle habits can support overall health and wellbeing.

  • Eat a balanced and nutritious diet.
  • Maintain regular physical activity.
  • Get adequate sleep.
  • Practice stress-management activities.
  • Maintain a healthy lifestyle.
  • Monitor menstrual cycles and symptoms.
  • Follow advice from a healthcare professional.

🩺 When Should You Consult a Doctor?

Consider speaking with a healthcare professional if you experience:

  • Very irregular or missing periods.
  • Persistent acne or excessive hair growth.
  • Significant unexplained changes in weight.
  • Difficulty becoming pregnant.
  • New or persistent symptoms that concern you.
