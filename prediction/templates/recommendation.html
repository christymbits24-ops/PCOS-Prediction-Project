⚠️ High Risk / PMOS Positive

🥗 Diet Recommendations

  • Include plenty of vegetables in your daily meals.
  • Choose whole grains such as oats, brown rice and whole wheat.
  • Include protein such as eggs, fish, chicken, beans and lentils.
  • Choose healthy fats such as nuts, seeds and avocado.
  • Choose fresh fruits instead of sweets and sugary snacks.
  • Limit highly processed and fried foods.
  • Limit foods and drinks containing large amounts of added sugar.
  • Try to maintain regular meal timings.
  • Drink enough water throughout the day.

🏃 Exercise Plan

  • Aim for around 30 minutes of physical activity on most days.
  • Monday: 30 minutes brisk walking.
  • Tuesday: 20–30 minutes strength exercises.
  • Wednesday: 30 minutes walking or cycling.
  • Thursday: 20–30 minutes yoga or stretching.
  • Friday: 30 minutes brisk walking.
  • Saturday: 20–30 minutes strength exercises.
  • Sunday: Rest or light walking.

😴 Sleep & Stress Management

  • Maintain a consistent sleep schedule.
  • Get adequate sleep every night.
  • Practice stress-management activities such as meditation or yoga.
  • Take short movement breaks if sitting for long periods.
  • Maintain a healthy daily routine.

💧 Hydration

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Choose water as your main everyday beverage.
  • Limit sugary drinks and soft drinks.

📋 Health Monitoring

  • Keep track of your menstrual cycle.
  • Monitor changes in weight and general wellbeing.
  • Record changes in PCOS-related symptoms.
  • Keep track of irregular periods or other changes.
  • Discuss persistent or concerning symptoms with a healthcare professional.
